### 🔍 What I Learned

- **Database-Level vs. ORM Cascades:** `Relationship(cascade_delete=True)` manages python objects in memory, while `Field(ondelete="CASCADE")` instructs PostgreSQL to enforce foreign key cascading at the database layer.
- **Explicit Driver Matching in Migrations:** Alembic's `env.py` needs explicit access to the application's engine or formatted `postgresql+psycopg://` connection URL to execute autogenerated migrations successfully.
- **Timezone Safety:** Standardizing on `TIMESTAMP(timezone=True)` (`TIMESTAMPTZ` in PostgreSQL) with server-side defaults (`server_default=text("CURRENT_TIMESTAMP")`) avoids timezone drift across distributed environments.

#### Problem

#### What I Tried
